STARS cursor data in dcb.bin.zst and keyboard.bin.zst is converted from
REDS stars/assets/cursors.go.

That source identifies VICE stars/cursor-bitmaps.go (STARS PCF-derived
cursor glyphs) and VICE's foreground/mask compositing logic. The active DCB
pointer was generated from the basic and basic_m glyphs in olcursor.bdf
(Sun OPEN LOOK), as documented in the REDS source.

Original source notices and licenses continue to apply to the asset data.
Converter and binary layout implementation: cmd/reds2stars/bitmap.go.
